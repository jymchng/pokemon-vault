# 07 — Wishlist & Rewards

## Wishlist

Save products from the store with the heart button, then review them on
`/wishlist`. Add to cart or remove items directly from the list.

![Wishlist](images/24-wishlist.png)

## Rewards

`/rewards` shows your **Collector Level** (XP from purchases and pack
openings) and the **Reward Ladder** — tiers unlock as you earn XP. XP is
computed and stored server-side.

![Rewards](images/25-rewards.png)
